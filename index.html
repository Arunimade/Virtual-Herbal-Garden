About the Initiative

The AYUSH Ministry

AYUSH — Ayurveda, Yoga & Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha, and Homeopathy — represents India's rich legacy of traditional medicine systems with over 5,000 years of documented history.

This Virtual Herbal Garden initiative brings ancient botanical knowledge to the digital age, making it accessible, engaging, and scientifically enriching for students, researchers, and practitioners worldwide.
